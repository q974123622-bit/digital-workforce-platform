# Onboarding Readiness

Organize 证据类别 into personnel record, account and access setup, device or service readiness, and policy acknowledgement.

## 完成条件

Completion requires evidence for every required category, a clear owner for any follow-up, and an auditable review result. A category with unavailable or conflicting data remains 未知; it is not inferred from another category.

State the final decision as ready, not ready, or incomplete with the missing evidence listed.
