# Onboarding Readiness

Organize 证据类别 into personnel record, account and access setup, device or service readiness, and policy acknowledgement.

For a general onboarding-readiness goal, 首轮 uses two primary evidence streams: HR obtains personnel and acknowledgement evidence, while IT obtains account, access, device, and service evidence. These streams may run independently.

独立审计属于二次复核，不是默认的根任务并列工作流。只有持有一手证据的 Worker 通过实际核查发现来源、完整性或留痕需要独立复核时，才从当前团队能力目录选择合适的协作者并派发受限子任务。

## 完成条件

Completion requires evidence for every required category, a clear owner for any follow-up, and an auditable review result. A category with unavailable or conflicting data remains 未知; it is not inferred from another category.

The Manager reviews the HR and IT direct-child summaries, including any nested review evidence, before making the completion decision.

State the final decision as ready, not ready, or incomplete with the missing evidence listed.
